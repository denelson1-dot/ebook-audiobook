What these files are

Raw audio, one piece at a time

Every sentence is narrated separately and saved uncompressed, then stitched into chapters and packed into the .m4b. The raw pieces stay behind afterwards.

All they buy you is a shortcut

While they exist, changing a voice setting re-narrates only the parts that changed — minutes instead of hours. Once you are happy with a book, that shortcut is worth nothing.

You keep everything that matters

The finished audiobook, your ebook, your voices, your section choices — untouched. You can always narrate the book again from scratch.
