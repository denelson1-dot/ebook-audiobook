How hard to work this machine

A full render runs for hours. If you want to keep using this computer while it does — or you're on a laptop that gets hot and loud — turn it down. The audiobook is identical either way; only the time it takes changes. You can override this for any single conversion.

Back up your books

Rendered audio is left out by default. It is reproducible from the book and your voice settings, and it is typically a thousand times larger than the work it surrounds — so including it turns a 30 MB backup into a 3 GB one for nothing. Choose Everything only if re-rendering would cost you more than the disk space.
